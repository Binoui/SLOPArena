# Manki aerial: leg correction after user review

The user correctly identified an anatomical problem in the refined aerial. The previous review missed it. The refinement raised the knees above the hips, reversed their bend plane and rolled the ankles sideways. Retained first-pass keys at ticks 21/25 also briefly interrupted the new tuck. The result met the fist/ankle height target while producing an implausible leg pose.

The separate corrected Unity candidate is `Assets/Art/Experiments/GenRAMankiAerial20261014/LegCorrectedDoubleFistAerial.anim` (GUID `77a9179731e7d203099cceb2965bae8f`, fileID `7400000`). The prior candidates, both GenRA model responses and all sparse model data remain intact. This was a manual correction with zero additional model calls.

`repair-legs.py` uses the real bind transforms and limb lengths to build the character's anatomical coordinate frame. It reflects the raised ankle targets below their own hips, chooses the correct knee-bend branch with explicit knee poles and restores the first pass's mild ankle rotations. The six thigh/calf/foot joints blend into this correction over the load, hold through the active strike and return smoothly to the accepted motion. The conflicting intermediate leg poses no longer interrupt the tuck.

Only six joints change, on ticks 7–38: 190 dense rotation samples. Every other rotation, every local translation/scale, the hierarchy and bind/rest matrices are preserved. All upper-body world matrices are exactly identical in USD. Frames 0–6 and 39–62, including entry, IASA and endpoint, are untouched. Duration remains 62 ticks, contact 23, active 23–30 and IASA 57. No negative mesh/bone scale, gameplay displacement or timing change was used.

Across the corrected interval, knee flexion is 135.9–145.9 degrees and the bend direction stays consistent. At contact the knees are 17.5 cm / 16.7 cm below their hips in the actual Unity preview. The heels sit lower and behind the strike: the previous +12.1 cm fist/ankle clearance becomes −14.1 cm. That silhouette metric was deliberately relinquished to fix the anatomy; the upper-body downward strike is unchanged.

The existing validated USD → ordinary FBX → root-baked Humanoid plus original-path FK pipeline was reused, including the original Manki Avatar and static runtime-origin calibration. The candidate has 570 curves, 60 fps, 62 baked frames and 1× speed. Actual Animancer playback agrees with prefab sampling within 0.357 micrometres. Root travel and root-scale change are zero; hip drift is below 0.11 micrometres. The 54 upper-body/tail bones differ from the accepted refinement by at most 0.131 micrometres in Unity. Entry/IASA/end comparisons are identical within the recorded float data.

Fall entry/IASA still match the currently playing reference within 0.219 mm, and the presentation-only 12-tick landing fades complete at interruption ticks 12/23/35. The remaining USD → Unity conversion residual is 2.088 mm on a toe-end helper. This is reported separately from the corrected bend direction. The existing Ability Lab catalog-hash blocker and pending gameplay/contact acceptance remain unchanged; this clip is unbound.

The close comparison at `artifacts/genra-manki-aerial-20261014/leg-corrected/comparison.html` shows previous and corrected motion at a shared clock, from an anatomical side angle and the gameplay-like angle. Normal-speed MP4s contain 62 frames at 60 fps; contact sheets cover 6/12/18/23/30/39/57. The reviewed mesh shows the knees folding below the hips with feet following the calves, rather than folding up toward the torso. Rear-view fist visibility remains the earlier upper-body issue and was outside this repair.

This is a useful control-rig requirement for future GenRA work: target positions alone admit the wrong leg solution. Knee poles, hinge limits and ankle-roll limits need to constrain the pose proposal and interpolation, alongside the existing timing and displacement checks.
