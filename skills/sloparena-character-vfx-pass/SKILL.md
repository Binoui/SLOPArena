# Character VFX pass

Presentation only. Effects and sounds follow authoritative semantic events and never
decide damage, hit results, timing or admission. In `character.json` only
`presentationIds`, `emitPresentation` operations and `hitPresentationId` change.
Damage, hitboxes and frame data stay as they are.

Reference pass: [Wibou](../../docs/characters/wibou.md#combat-presentation--2026-10-16),
generated by `client/Unity/Assets/Scripts/Editor/WibouPresentationBuilder.cs`. The
universal hit feel (shake, flash, squash) is separate and shared by every fighter; see
[Fighter hit feel](../../docs/systems/vfx-particles.md#fighter-hit-feel). Scope, order
and open decisions live in the
[combat presentation plan](../../docs/plans/2026-10-16-combat-presentation-pass.md).

## 1. Confirm the identity first

1. Ask the owner for one sentence: who the character is now, the joke, and what to
   avoid. Older concept sections can be stale; Wibou's kitsune and foxfire concept was
   retired on 2026-10-17 after a pass had been built on it. Record the answer at the top
   of `docs/characters/<package>.md` before designing.
2. Choose two or three recurring motifs, one accent colour, a dark rim colour and a
   white or pale core.
3. Inventory what exists: `Presentation/` assets, `emitPresentation` cues,
   `hitPresentationId` per slot, the weapon trail style in the catalog's weapon config,
   entries in `Resources/VFXConfigs/ProjectileVisuals.asset`, and sounds.

## 2. Capture "before"

From stopped Edit Mode with saved scenes and an open Game View:

```bash
python3 tools/presentation_review.py batch --attacker <package> --victim <other> \
  --stage splash_deck --output tmp/vfx/<package>/before > tmp/vfx/<package>/before.json
bun scripts/unity-editor-gateway.ts --project-path client/Unity \
  --batch tmp/vfx/<package>/before.json > tmp/vfx/<package>/before.out
python3 tools/presentation_review.py summary tmp/vfx/<package>/before.out
```

Repeat with `--stage after_hours` and another output folder. Bright Splash Deck exposes
glow that washes out; dark After Hours exposes muddy contrast. Keep the summary's hit
frames: the "after" run must land the same hits. Defaults per slot are starting points.
Use `--set A:distance=4` for projectile range (the rig allows 0.4–4 m), `--set 4:ticks=110`
for long startups and `--set A:hold=52` to fully charge an aim-hold special. The rig aims at
the fighter spacing, so a ground-cursor special (Manki's Round Bomb) lands on the victim
once it reads the cursor: give it a short hold (`--set A:hold=12`).
The wide camera follows both fighters, which keeps leaps and launches in frame.

Then show the owner a concept board before building: paint the proposed effects over
these "before" frames with the same banding the builder bakes, one panel per beat, with
short captions and a palette. Wait for approval; concepts are cheap to change, built
effects are not. Keep gags at the character's expense, and keep finishers dignified if the
owner asks (FightGuy's Fist of Fury).

## 3. Build the effects

Create `<Name>PresentationBuilder.cs` next to Wibou's, with
`[CliCommand("sloparena.presentation.<package>.build")]`. Use
`PresentationBuilderKit` for assets, prefabs, sounds and bindings, and import
`PresentationFx` and `ToonShapes` with `using static`. Copy Wibou's structure: palette,
materials, one method per prefab, then the bindings and `kit.Finish(built)`. Name IDs
`presentation.<package>.<name>` and list them in `presentationIds`.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Bake shapes with `ToonShapes.Bands`: dark rim, accent, white core. Materials are alpha-blended; reserve `additive: true` for soft halos drawn `Behind`. | Additive glow washes out to white on bright stages. |
| Bone-attached cues use attack-bound roots and the default Local scaling. | Weapon bones carry large import scales; attack-bound cues end on interruption. |
| `hitPresentationId` effects use Hierarchy scaling and `InFront`. | Contacts are scaled by damage (`clamp(sqrt(damage/6), 0.6, 1.6)`) and must draw over trails. |
| World-placed cue offsets start at the capsule centre (`PY`), not the feet. | Wibou's centre is 0.85 m above the feet; the first pass floated. |
| A cue's `durationTicks` covers its longest particle and its sound. | The dispatcher destroys the instance and its `AudioSource` when the duration ends. |
| Aim-hold capabilities freeze the stage clock: tick 0 fires at charge start, tick 1 right after release. | Charge-tier feedback needs another trigger; see the plan's open decisions. |
| A weapon trail style is a prefab: root ParticleSystem material, tint and fade, plus optional `Dots`, `Lines` and `Background slash` children. Bind it with `kit.BindWeaponTrail`. | `SwordSweepTrail` reads only these. |
| `ProjectileVFXManager.OrientProjectile` turns the root's +Y along the flight, for Wibou's slot A only. Measure a model's tip axis and rotate it inside the prefab; other slots need that method extended. | Wibou's kunai flew ring-first because its FBX stores the blade diagonally. |
| Clamp before fractional powers (`Mathf.Pow(Mathf.Max(0f, Mathf.Sin(...)), 0.75f)`). | `Sin(π)` is slightly negative; NaN vertices give "Invalid AABB" asserts. |
| Put bone-attached content under `PresentationFrame` (unit scale, optionally upright). | Rigs carry import scales (FightGuy's bones are 1.5×), which scale offsets and local-space particles. |
| Give each new MonoBehaviour its own file named after the class. | Otherwise the prefab saves a missing script and the component silently does nothing; `kit.Save` now throws. |
| Attack ticks keep counting while an aim-hold charges, and Hitstop pauses them. | Give charge cues a long `durationTicks`, and find late cues several frames after the nominal tick in captures. |
| Read cosmetic state through `IPresentationOwnerReceiver`: `ChargeTierCues` (up to five tiers, an `AudioSource` per tier effect, a `Grow` transform scaled per tier), `ChargeTierVariant`, `DamageScaleVariant` (also reads a placement's charge scale). | Charge-tier pings, rare full-charge variants and finisher looks need no Shared change. |
| A stretched billboard's head is the particle and its quad trails behind it, with U = 0 at the head. Draw a flame's tip at U = 0, keep `lengthScale` near 1.2 with `velocityScale` 0, spawn slightly ahead and grow the size with distance. | Manki's first flame stream trailed 2.5 m backward, over Manki, from particles that were moving forward. |
| A world-space trail inside an attack-bound cue vanishes when the attack ends. Put lingering trails in their own cue without `AttackBoundPresentation`, and mark projectile trails with `LingerAfterProjectile`. | Manki's jetpack puff column ends at the apex with the move; his rocket's smoke must outlive the rocket. |
| Put floor marks (rings, scorches, debris landing planes) under a `SnapToFloor` child; particles can collide with that plane. | Blasts happen mid-air; the stage floor comes from `PresentationFloor`, and no floor below hides the mark. |
| Measure prop anchors with a read-only `eval_file` that attaches the prop as `WeaponAttach` does and reports points in the hand or cue frame. | Manki's bazooka barrel runs along the muzzle cue's +Y and his spray can's nozzle sits at the hand's −Z; guessing them points effects the wrong way. |
| Use `ToonShapes.Step` for shader-style smoothstep. | `Mathf.SmoothStep(a, b, t)` interpolates from a to b; it is not a 0–1 step. |
| Delete prefabs, textures and materials you stop generating. | The builder overwrites, it never removes. |

Glyph and logo art: render a mask with `tools/glyph_mask.py` into `Presentation/Source/`
and bake it with `new MaskField(path)` and `ToonShapes.Bands`, as FightGuy's 肉 does.

Sounds come from `kit.Sound(root, clip, volume, pitch)`. Clips live in the ignored
`Resources/Audio/SFX/<Name>/` folder. Trim and normalize them (about −3 dB peak), and
record source and licence in [asset notes](../../docs/assets/credits-and-licenses.md)
and `CREDITS.md`. Script the mix so another machine can rebuild the ignored clips, as
`tools/sfx/manki_clips.py` does from Kenney's CC0 packs and generated noise. Models nested from `Assets/Art` are local-only too; the kit warns when
one is missing.

## 4. Rebuild, cook and verify

Run one gateway batch: `recompile`, `clear_console`,
`sloparena.presentation.<package>.build`, `sloparena.character.cook --target <package>`,
`sloparena.character.verify --target <package>`, then `get_console_logs` for errors and
warnings. Check it with `presentation_review.py summary`.

- Recompile before any refresh after C# edits in the shared Editor.
- If another thread's change breaks compilation, wait; do not edit their files.
- Rebuilds are byte-identical for unchanged inputs. Hash `Presentation/` before and after
  a refactor to prove it changed nothing.
- A cook re-pins the roster manifest; stage only your package's pin. Saving the catalog
  auto-cooks the roster, so another package with a stale cook (for example after a cooker
  version change) is recooked too; leave that change to its owner.
- Big capsule hitboxes report their contact on the hitbox surface toward the target, up to a
  radius away from a victim deep inside; a hit effect there pops in mid-air (see the plan's
  open decisions).
- Builder materials and textures match the global `*.mat` and `*.png` ignore rules.
  Re-include your package's prefix in `.gitignore`, as FightGuy, Wibou and Manki do, then run
  `python3 tools/check_asset_closure.py` on the package catalog: only the local audio
  may be reported missing.

## 5. Capture "after" and review

Rerun the step 2 batches into `after` folders. `summary` must show the same hit frames.

```bash
python3 tools/presentation_review.py sheet tmp/vfx/<package>/after/4 --frames 30-60:3 --out tmp/vfx/<package>/sheets/4.jpg
python3 tools/presentation_review.py clips --set TODAY=tmp/vfx/<package>/before \
  --set NEW=tmp/vfx/<package>/after --moves 1,4@28+48,A --out tmp/vfx/<package>/page
python3 tools/presentation_review.py page tmp/vfx/<package>/page --spec tmp/vfx/<package>/page.json
```

- Inspect sheets yourself first, with full-resolution crops for small effects; check both
  stages and the wide view for leaps and launches.
- Show the owner close-ups at ¼× and 1×, and option grids for open choices. Gameplay-distance
  comparisons hide these effects.
- Keep a page under about 20 MB: lower `--quality` or shorten the frame windows.
- If you already changed the package, recapture "before" by importing the `HEAD` versions of
  `character.json` and the catalog with `import_asset`, then cook and capture. Restore your
  versions in the batch's `restore` list, which runs even after a failure, and check their hashes.

## 6. Document

- The character doc gets a "Combat presentation" section with one row per move.
- The current-state table in [VFX and particles](../../docs/systems/vfx-particles.md).
- The plan's status and open decisions.
- Credits for any new sound or art.
- Owner checks in the local, ignored `TESTING-UNITY.md`.

The pass is done when the owner has approved it in game, cook and verify are valid with
a clean console, the "after" captures land the "before" hits, and the docs match.
